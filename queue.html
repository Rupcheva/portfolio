About Queue

Queue is an AI-powered startup platform that helps users create content through a voice interview with AI. It’s designed for individuals looking to amplify their presence and engage effectively on social networks.

AI Voice Interview

Queue leverages an AI named Jessica to conduct voice interviews, helping users generate personalized content effortlessly.

Social Media Content Creation

Users receive two weeks’ worth of ready-to-post content tailored for LinkedIn and other social networks, focusing on industry-relevant topics.

AI-Driven Topic Discovery

Queue analyzes audience interests and trends, helping users identify the most relevant and timely topics to engage their network.

☹️ Problem Statement

Interview List Accessibility

Users are struggling to locate their interview list, requiring them to manually save links to revisit their interviews. This hampers their efficiency and control over their content management.

Content Scheduling Feature Development

Users want to schedule content publishing to save time and eliminate the need for manual posting every week. Currently, no such functionality exists on competing platforms, making it challenging for users to manage their content efficiently.

Visual Content Enhancement

Users seek to enhance their posts with personalized or humorous images that complement their text, but they lack an efficient way to find suitable visuals without significant time investment.

AI Tone of Voice Consistency

Users are dissatisfied with AI-generated posts that do not reflect their unique tone of voice, leading to additional editing efforts before publishing. They desire a solution that allows the AI to accurately replicate their personal style in generated content.

🎯 Goal

Develop a solution that allows users to access all their interviews in one consolidated location, facilitating easy navigation and management, ultimately aiming to enhance user retention.

Develop a content scheduling feature that allows users to plan and automate their publishing tasks, enabling them to save time and increase the frequency of their posts without the hassle of manual updates.

Create methods for users to incorporate personalized images and illustrations into their posts, including the creation of carousels on LinkedIn, with the goal of increasing engagement and interaction with their content.

Find ways to personalize users’ tone of voice in AI-generated content, ensuring that the output requires no edits before publication, thus streamlining the content creation process.

Solution

Dashboard Development

I designed the dashboard and conversational experience from the ground up, bringing AI-generated quotes, podcast clips, and social drafts into a single workspace.

Content Scheduling Feature Development

Creators can plan and schedule posts in the dashboard, then review upcoming publications in a calendar.

Enhanced Visual Content Creation with AI

Creators can personalize templates and generate images for their LinkedIn posts.

Smart Tone of Voice Personalization Using AI

I designed an AI flow that uses a writer’s input to adapt generated posts to their individual tone of voice.
