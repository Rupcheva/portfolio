Transformative Redesign of SAAS Platform:
Process description

About Instapanel

Instapanel is a B2B SaaS market research platform supporting 90% of employees' daily work, from study proposals and audience targeting to participant recruitment and data management.

My Role

I was the sole Senior Product Designer at Instapanel, leading the end-to-end redesign of a B2B SaaS platform in collaboration with C-level leadership and Product.

Simplified workflows, contributing to 15% revenue growth.

Reduced proposal creation from 18 to 7 minutes and participant search from 45 minutes to ~3 seconds.

Redesigned information architecture, reducing core task completion time by 40%.

Prioritized MVP features, conducted usability testing, and delivered validated designs.

Problem Statement

Instapanel's SAAS platform faced challenges due to outdated design and lack of intuitiveness:

  • Learning Curve: Users struggled with a non-intuitive interface, requiring extensive tutorials.
  • Redundant Actions: Inefficiencies led to repeated tasks, increasing frustration and task durations.
  • User Experience: Overall, the outdated design impacted productivity and satisfaction negatively.

In summary, Instapanel's platform demands a complete UX redesign to address the learning curve, eliminate redundant actions, and enhance user experience.

Project Challenges

Redesigning the core product areas while preserving familiar workflows for an expert B2B audience.

Pricing Tables

Problem

  1. Employees used Google Sheets to create pricing tables.
  2. Each client request required a new pricing table, making the workflow time-consuming.
  3. Estimating the right price often required help from a manager.

Solution

Users can effortlessly generate pricing tables within the platform once the audience is created, eliminating the need for Google Sheets.

The Smart Pricing table now automatically suggests pricing based on entered criteria and audience details, eliminating any confusion regarding pricing decisions.

With the new system, users can edit the table and generate multiple options in less than 30 seconds. Smart table suggests all needed options.

Result

Screening

Simplifying complex participant criteria without losing the flexibility needed by research teams.

Problem

  1. Users needed to link the right questionnaire question to each screening criterion.
  2. Users needed a clear way to screen out panelists who did not meet the questionnaire criteria.

Solution

Users can choose a question from the list and select the corresponding screening answer.

Result
