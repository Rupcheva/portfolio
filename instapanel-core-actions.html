Case Study: Enhancing Core Actions
for Time Savings

My Role

I was the sole Senior Product Designer at Instapanel, leading the end-to-end redesign of a B2B SaaS platform in collaboration with C-level leadership and Product.

The redesign simplified core workflows, reducing proposal creation from 18 to 7 minutes and panelist search from 45 minutes to 3 seconds, improving efficiency and contributing to revenue growth.

The Case in One Minute

Context
Instapanel’s internal platform helps client strategists manage research studies, define audiences, and recruit participants. Inefficient workflows forced employees to rely on external tools.
Problem
Creating proposals took 18 minutes, while manually finding 100 suitable panelists took around 45 minutes.
Solution
I introduced an integrated proposal editor with structured audience criteria, enabling automated participant matching and CSV export.
Impact
Proposal creation time dropped by 61%, while participant search and export took just 3 seconds in two clicks, freeing up time for client work and research.

After client approval, strategists recruit people who match the criteria, prioritising those who have participated in previous studies.

3 Key findings:

Two Sources of Wasted Time

Problem Statement and Key Metrics:

1. Time to write the proposal and transfer it to the platform: 18 min

The need to draft proposals in external tools like Google Docs before transferring them to the SaaS platform contributes to user frustration, inefficiency and doubles the time.

2. Time to find 100 panelists: 45 min

Users struggle to find panelists meeting specific quotas and criteria within Instapanel’s database.

The current process demands significant time investment, as users must navigate through previous proposals to locate similar panelists.

Solution and Hypothesis Test

What Users Needed:

Create a Proposal once

Users wanted to write proposals directly in the platform instead of creating them in Google Docs and retyping the same information.

Their goal was to remove duplicate work and spend less time setting up each study.

Find existing participants easily

Strategists knew suitable participants were already in Instapanel’s database, but finding them required searching through previous projects.

They wanted to filter participants by the audience criteria and quickly access their contact details.

Keep the familiar proposal structure

Users were already comfortable describing audience requirements in sentences. The new editor needed to preserve that familiar structure while making criteria such as age, location and income usable by the platform.

Key research insight

The interviews highlighted an opportunity to connect proposal creation with participant search: enter the audience requirements once, then reuse them to find matching people.

1 Analyse and categorise existing proposals written in Google Docs

2 Ideation of Solution

  • Introduce a text writing feature enabling users to craft proposals seamlessly within the platform.
  • Implement natural language forms for enabling the platform in identifying fitting panelists based on filled data and also for document consistency, suggesting optimal structures.
  • Natural language forms enhance user experience by allowing users to write the Proposal using familiar language, making the input process more intuitive.
    They streamline data entry, ensuring consistency, and enable the system to understand and respond to user inputs more effectively.
  • Implement a feature that allows users to view the number of available panelists who match the specified criteria in the database and provides the option to download the information in CSV format.

3 User Testing of Developed feature:

6 testing sessions with Client Strategists

The beta version of the Audience creation feature has been developed by our team of developers. I conducted extensive testing with Instapanel employees, gathering valuable feedback and insights. Now, armed with this input, I am ready to proceed with the creation of the final high fidelity mockups.

What Didn’t Work

Initial Idea

I designed proposal creation to work like a text document, aiming for 95% keyboard use. Magic inputs recognised audience criteria as users typed, with no dropdowns and minimal clicks.

What didn’t work

Testing with 10 users showed that the interaction wasn’t clear:

  • 6 struggled to use it.
  • 4 completed the task, but still found it difficult.

Users wanted mouse controls and a clearer structure. Adoption was particularly challenging for users aged 35+.

What I Changed

I added dropdowns with clear criteria options and let users add new lines with the mouse.

Result

8 out of 10 users created a proposal on their first attempt. User satisfaction also improved.

Lesson Learned

Start with familiar interactions and introduce complexity when it helps users. A novel idea still needs to be easy to understand.

Deliver

The final workflow connects proposal creation, panelist search and screening.

  1. Proposal creation. Write proposals directly in Instapanel.
  2. Panelist data. Export details for matching panelists in two clicks.
  3. Clear structure. Use suggestions to guide strategists through the editor.
  4. Less duplicate work. Create proposals in the platform without transferring them from Google Docs.
  5. Panel setup. Move directly from the proposal to screening and the rest of the panel workflow.

How I Measured Success

Proposal Creation Time

I timed employees in live sessions before and after the redesign. Proposal creation decreased from 18 to 7 minutes, about a 61% reduction.

Finding Existing Panelists

The original task involved searching past panels by criteria such as age, location and income. The three-second measurement started after users entered the proposal criteria and covered viewing matches and downloading the CSV.

Task Completion and User Feedback

Users shared more positive comments during testing and team meetings. The feedback was qualitative.
