About Ureeka

Ureeka is a US-based startup connecting small business owners and underrepresented entrepreneurs with 200+ mentors. We built and launched an iOS and desktop networking platform to support mentorship, engagement, and business growth, contributing to $8.6M in funding.

My Role

As a Senior Product Designer on a team of four, I helped build Ureeka from 0 to 1, owning the end-to-end design of all features showcased in this case study across iOS and web. The product successfully launched in the US and attracted $8M in funding.

  • Worked as a Senior Product Designer within a team of four designers, helping build and launch the product from 0 to 1.

  • Owned the end-to-end design of key features across iOS and web, from user research and testing to final UI and launch.

  • Built a scalable design system, ensuring consistency across platforms.

  • Collaborated with Product, Marketing, and Engineering to align user needs with business goals.

Design Process

  1. Requirements Gathering

    Collaborate with business and product teams to collect project requirements and tasks.

  2. Ideation and Research

    Conduct UX research, including mobile-first design, competitor research, and low-fidelity wireframes.

  3. User Flow Design

    Create flowcharts and mid-fidelity user flows to visualize the product’s structure.

  4. Concept Presentation

    Present design concepts to the project team for discussion and feedback.

  5. Prototyping

    Develop clickable prototypes for interactive testing and user feedback.

  6. User Testing

    Conduct user testing sessions to gather insights and refine the design.

  7. High-Fidelity Design and Development Support

    Finalize design flows with rules and explanations for developers.

  8. Launch and Iteration

    Launch the product and initiate ongoing feedback and iteration cycles for continuous improvement.
